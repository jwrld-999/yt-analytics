YT AI Intel

YT AI Intel is a personal automation that builds a weekly report on YouTube trends in the AI and automation niche. It uses public YouTube data and saves the report to the owner's own Google Drive as a Google Slides deck and a Google Sheet. It then emails the report to the owner using Gmail.

It is a private tool used only by its developer. It is not offered to the public.
